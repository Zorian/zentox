The Protocol — open & documented

Five Steps.
Nothing Hidden.

This is our exact treatment plan, published in full. Every property follows it, every stage is recorded, and you receive the documentation at sign-off. The last step is the one most contractors skip — it's why the mould doesn't come back.

STATUS: OPEN
METHOD: PUBLISHED
AUDIT: PER PROPERTY
STEP01
Survey & Diagnose
A CSRT-qualified surveyor maps every affected area and finds the root cause — condensation, penetrating or rising damp, or a leak. Full moisture readings and photo evidence go into a written diagnostic report that satisfies the Awaab's Law 10-day investigation.
OUTPUT: Written survey + moisture map + photo log
STEP02
Contain & Strip
We isolate the work area to stop spores spreading through the home, then remove what can't be saved — flaking paint, blown plaster, contaminated wallpaper. The space is protected so the tenant can stay safe throughout.
CONTROL: Spore containment · safe removal of affected materials
STEP03
Kill & Clean
We treat every affected surface with an HSE-approved fungicidal wash that destroys mould, mildew and algae at the source and lifts the biofilm they grow from — not just the visible black staining on top.
PRODUCT: Benzalkonium chloride fungicidal wash · HSE-approved surface biocide
STEP04
Repair & Ventilate
Killing the mould isn't enough if the cause is still there. We make good — re-plaster, treat the structure, fix the defect — and improve ventilation or insulation so the conditions that grew the mould are gone for good.
ROOT CAUSE: Make-good · ventilation / extraction · moisture control
STEP05
Protect & Sign-Off
We finish with a residual antifungal barrier — an invisible protective treatment that keeps inhibiting growth long after we leave, so the mould can't simply return. Then we verify the work and hand you a complete compliance pack, ready for Housing Ombudsman scrutiny.
PRODUCT: Residual biocidal barrier — benzalkonium chloride + 2-phenylphenol · inhibits regrowth
OUTPUT: Full sign-off documentation pack
What We Use

The Bottle,
On the Label.

Most contractors won't tell you what they spray in your tenants' homes. We will. Our treatment uses professional, HSE-approved biocides — the same active ingredients trusted across the UK trade — applied at the correct strength by trained operatives.

They're low-odour, biodegradable, and safe for occupied homes when used as directed. If a tenant or surveyor asks what's been applied, you have the answer in writing.

✓ HSE-approved · safe in occupied homes
Stage 1 · Kill & clean
Benzalkonium Chloride
A quaternary-ammonium fungicidal wash. Destroys mould, mildew and algae on contact and breaks down the organic film they grow from.
Stage 2 · Protect & prevent
BAC + 2-Phenylphenol
A residual biocidal barrier left on the treated surface. Keeps working after application to inhibit regrowth — the step that stops mould coming back.
